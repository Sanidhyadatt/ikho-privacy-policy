1. Overview & Data Scope

IKHO does not require user accounts, registration, or logins, and MistyForgeLabs does not operate backend servers or integrate analytics or crash-reporting trackers. Data handling in IKHO separates cleanly into two areas:

Component Where Processing Happens Network Required
Voice capture, ASR, translation (EN ↔ JA), and TTS On your device using bundled ONNX & Sherpa-ONNX models No (100% offline; zero audio or text sent to MistyForgeLabs)
Banner advertising & regional consent prompts Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK & Google UMP SDK Yes, only when internet is available and consent rules allow

2. Offline AI Processing

Microphone Audio & Speech Recognition (ASR)

When you tap the microphone button, IKHO captures 16 kHz mono PCM audio in device memory (RAM) for local Voice Activity Detection (Silero VAD) and speech-to-text transcription (SenseVoice ONNX models). Recorded audio stays on your device, is never saved to permanent storage by IKHO, and is discarded once transcription completes or recording is cancelled.

Text & Neural Machine Translation (NMT)

Typed text and recognized speech are translated between English and Japanese on your phone using bundled quantized MarianMT ONNX models and an offline phrase fallback. Text inputs and translations are not sent to MistyForgeLabs servers or cloud translation APIs on Android.

Text-to-Speech Playback (TTS)

Spoken translations are synthesized offline using bundled Piper VITS (English) and Melo-VITS (Japanese) models. During playback, synthesized speech is written to a temporary .wav file inside the app’s temporary cache directory and deleted automatically when you play another clip or close the player. If native ONNX speech synthesis is unavailable for a phrase, IKHO calls Android’s built-in system Text-to-Speech service locally as a fallback.

Session History, Settings & Clipboard

Conversation messages displayed in the Translate console and History tab, along with active session settings (UI language, speech rate, default direction, and script auto-detection), are held in device memory for the active session. Text is copied to your device clipboard only when you tap Copy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Google UMP Consent Management

IKHO integrates the official google_mobile_ads Flutter plugin (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform SDK) to display anchored adaptive banner ads. IKHO does not include interstitial or rewarded ads.

Consent Flow Before Loading Ads

  • On startup (and resume if not yet completed), IKHO calls Google UMP (ConsentInformation.requestConsentInfoUpdate) to check regional privacy requirements such as EEA/UK/Switzerland GDPR and regulated US state rules.
  • Where required by regulation and configured in AdMob, IKHO presents Google’s consent message (loadAndShowConsentFormIfRequired) before initializing the Mobile Ads SDK or requesting ads.
  • If UMP does not allow ad requests (canRequestAds() is false), or if the device is offline before consent verification succeeds, IKHO keeps Mobile Ads uninitialized and displays no banner ads.
  • IKHO hides and disposes banner ads whenever microphone recording, speech recognition, translation, or audio playback is active.

Data Processed by Google AdMob & Partners

When UMP permits ad requests and the device is online, Google Mobile Ads and its advertising/mediation partners may collect or process:

  • Advertising & device identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, and Privacy Sandbox AdServices signals where supported by Android.
  • Network & approximate location: IP address, used by Google to infer general geographic region and verify consent rules.
  • Ad interaction & diagnostic data: Ad impressions, clicks, request timing, OS/device metadata, and SDK performance or fraud-prevention diagnostics.

Depending on your region and consent choice, Google may serve personalized ads or limited/non-personalized ads. Details are governed by Google’s own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Android App Permissions

The table below lists the permissions present in IKHO’s release Android manifest and their exact purpose:

Permission Origin Purpose
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO IKHO (runtime prompt) Records microphone audio only during active voice translation; processed locally in RAM and never uploaded by IKHO.
android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS IKHO Manages local audio routing during speech capture and TTS playback.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Google Mobile Ads / UMP SDK Used by Google UMP and AdMob for consent checks and banner requests. Core translation features do not require network access.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID & ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* Google Mobile Ads SDK Allows Google AdMob to read the Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox signals subject to consent and OS controls.
WAKE_LOCK & FOREGROUND_SERVICE Google Mobile Ads / AndroidX WorkManager Merged automatically by Play Services / WorkManager dependencies used by the Ads SDK. IKHO does not run background recording services.

5. Storage, Backups & Deletion

  • Bundled AI models: 29 AI model files (~857 MB) are delivered via Google Play’s install-time AI pack and extracted locally to internal app storage (app_flutter/models).
  • Backup exclusions: IKHO configures backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml so the extracted models directory is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers, while standard OS preferences are preserved where applicable.
  • Local retention: MistyForgeLabs retains no copies of voice recordings or translations. In-session phrases remain only in memory until cleared or the app closes, and temporary TTS .wav cache files are cleaned up on subsequent playback or disposal.
  • How to delete data: Clear conversation history in-app via History → Clear or Settings → Clear All, clear Android app storage or uninstall IKHO to remove all local files and cached UMP preferences, or follow our dedicated Data Deletion & Ad Choices guide for full instructions and third-party retention limits.

6. Third-Party Services

MistyForgeLabs does not sell personal data and does not share microphone audio or translated text with third parties. The only third-party data-processing SDK integrated on Android is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google UMP, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Tapping Open Link inside Settings opens the URL in your device’s external browser.

7. Data Security

Speech recognition, translation, and speech synthesis run locally on your device without transmitting audio or text payloads over the internet. Extracted models reside inside Android’s private app sandbox, cleartext HTTP traffic is not enabled by IKHO in the Android manifest, and AdMob/UMP communications use encrypted network transport managed by Google’s SDKs.

8. Children’s Privacy

IKHO is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and MistyForgeLabs does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe third-party advertising SDKs processed data from a child without appropriate consent, contact us using the email below; you can also reset or delete your device Advertising ID and uninstall IKHO.

9. Your Privacy & Ad Choices

  • Ad Privacy Choices (Google UMP): Where regional laws require a consent-management entry point, open Settings → PRIVACY & LEGAL → Ad Privacy Choices inside IKHO to review, update, or withdraw ad consent.
  • Android Advertising ID: Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Microphone permission: Deny or revoke microphone access in Android Settings → Apps → IKHO → Permissions; typed translation and TTS playback continue to work without it.
  • Data Deletion Guide: See Data Deletion & Ad Choices for step-by-step local deletion instructions and Google AdMob retention disclosures.
