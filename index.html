Privacy Policy

IKHO Mobile Application

MistyForgeLabs (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built the IKHO Android application (com.ikho.mistyforgelabs). This Privacy Policy explains clearly how IKHO handles voice input, translated text, local storage, device permissions, and advertising via Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Offline Translation & Voice

Speech recognition, text translation, and voice synthesis run locally on your device using bundled on-device AI models. MistyForgeLabs does not operate backend servers and does not receive or store your voice recordings or translated phrases.

Advertising & Consent

IKHO displays banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Although core translation works offline without sending data to us, Google’s AdMob and UMP SDKs process advertising-related device data when network access is available and consent rules are satisfied.

1. Overview & Scope

This policy applies to the IKHO Android application distributed by MistyForgeLabs. IKHO does not require user accounts, logins, or registration, and MistyForgeLabs does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs or crash-reporting trackers.

Data handling in IKHO falls into two distinct categories:

  • On-device AI processing: Microphone capture, speech recognition, translation, and text-to-speech playback occur locally on your phone without transmitting audio or text to MistyForgeLabs servers.
  • Third-party advertising SDKs: The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK connect to Google servers over the internet to check consent requirements and load banner advertisements when allowed.

2. Offline AI Data Processing

Voice Audio & Speech Recognition (ASR)

When you initiate voice input, IKHO captures 16 kHz mono PCM audio using your device’s microphone and holds those audio samples temporarily in device memory (RAM) for Voice Activity Detection (Silero VAD) and speech-to-text recognition (SenseVoice ONNX models). Captured microphone audio is processed locally on your device, is not saved to permanent storage by IKHO, and is discarded once transcription finishes or recording is cancelled.

Text & Neural Machine Translation (NMT)

Typed text and transcribed speech are translated between English and Japanese on your device using pre-bundled quantized MarianMT ONNX neural models and an embedded offline phrase fallback. Your text and translations are never transmitted to MistyForgeLabs servers or cloud translation APIs on Android.

Text-to-Speech (TTS)

IKHO synthesizes spoken audio offline using bundled Piper VITS (English) and Melo-VITS (Japanese) models. During playback, synthesized audio is written to a temporary .wav file inside the app’s temporary cache directory and automatically deleted when you play another clip, stop playback, or close the player. If native ONNX speech synthesis is unavailable for an utterance, IKHO invokes your device’s built-in Android operating system Text-to-Speech service locally as a fallback.

Session History & In-App Settings

Translated messages shown in the console and History tab, along with session settings (UI language mode, playback rate, default translation direction, and script auto-detection toggle), are kept in memory for the active session. When you tap Copy on a translation or copy an external URL in Settings, that text is placed on your device’s local clipboard only upon your action.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Google UMP Consent Management

IKHO integrates the official google_mobile_ads Flutter plugin (Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform SDK) to display anchored adaptive banner advertisements. IKHO currently does not display interstitial or rewarded advertisements.

How Consent Works Before Ads Load

  • At app startup (and upon resuming if not yet updated), IKHO calls Google UMP (ConsentInformation.requestConsentInfoUpdate) to check regional privacy requirements (such as in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, where applicable).
  • Where required by regional regulations and configured in AdMob, IKHO presents Google’s consent form (loadAndShowConsentFormIfRequired) before initializing the Mobile Ads SDK or loading any banner ads.
  • If UMP consent requirements are not satisfied, or if the device is offline and consent cannot be verified, IKHO keeps ads disabled and does not initialize the Mobile Ads SDK or request banners.
  • To avoid interrupting voice and translation workflows, IKHO suppresses and hides banner ads whenever microphone recording, speech recognition, translation processing, or audio playback is active.

Data Collected or Processed by Google AdMob & Partners

When UMP consent permits ad requests and your device has internet connectivity, Google AdMob and its advertising/mediation partners may automatically collect and process data including:

  • Advertising & device identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), App Set ID, and Android Privacy Sandbox AdServices signals (where supported by your OS).
  • Network & coarse location data: IP address, which may be used by Google to estimate general/approximate location (such as country or city) and check regional consent.
  • Ad interaction & diagnostic metrics: Ad impressions, clicks, request latency, basic device metadata (OS version, device model, screen dimensions), and SDK error or performance diagnostics for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and ad measurement.

Depending on your region and consent choices, AdMob may serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Review Google’s policies for full details on how Google handles data in apps using its advertising SDKs:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Android App Permissions

Below is an accurate summary of permissions present in IKHO’s release Android manifest and why each is present:

Permission Source Purpose
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO IKHO (runtime requested) Captures microphone input only when you activate voice translation. Audio is processed on device in RAM and never transmitted to MistyForgeLabs servers.
android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS IKHO Configures device audio routing during local speech capture and audio playback.
android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Google Mobile Ads / UMP SDK Allows Google UMP consent updates and AdMob banner ad requests. Core translation, ASR, and TTS work completely offline without internet access.
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID & ACCESS_ADSERVICES_* Google Mobile Ads SDK Allows Google AdMob to access the Android Advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox AdServices (Ad ID, Attribution, Topics) for ad delivery and measurement, subject to your consent and device settings.
WAKE_LOCK & FOREGROUND_SERVICE Google Mobile Ads / AndroidX WorkManager Automatically merged by Google Mobile Ads SDK’s Play Services / WorkManager dependencies for standard SDK task management. IKHO itself does not run foreground recording services when closed.

5. Data Storage, Retention, Backups & Deletion

  • On-device AI models: The bundled 29 AI model files (~857 MB) are delivered at install time via Google Play’s install-time AI pack and extracted locally to internal app storage (app_flutter/models) for native ONNX Runtime and Sherpa-ONNX execution.
  • Android Backup Exclusion: IKHO configures explicit Android backup rules (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml) so the extracted models directory is excluded from Android Auto Backup cloud uploads and device-to-device transfers, while preserving standard OS-managed app preferences where applicable.
  • Retention: MistyForgeLabs retains zero copies of your voice recordings or translations. In-session phrases exist only in memory until cleared or the app terminates. Temporary synthesized TTS .wav cache files are deleted automatically after subsequent playback/disposal.
  • Data Deletion: You can clear in-session phrases immediately inside IKHO via History → Clear or Settings → Clear All, revoke microphone access in Android Settings, reset/delete your Android Advertising ID, or uninstall IKHO to remove all local app storage and extracted models from your device. For full instructions and retention limitations regarding Google AdMob/UMP data, see the dedicated Data Deletion Request page. For data held by Google AdMob, deletion and retention follow Google’s own privacy controls and policies.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

MistyForgeLabs does not sell your personal information and does not share your voice audio or translated text with any third party.

The only third-party data processing SDK integrated into IKHO on Android is:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used for regional privacy consent prompts and banner advertising. Data shared with or collected by this SDK is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

If you tap Open Link on a Privacy Policy URL inside Settings (when configured), your device opens your chosen external web browser application.

7. Data Security

Because speech recognition, translation, and neural voice synthesis occur on your device without sending voice or text payloads over the network, those payloads are not exposed to server-side storage or developer server breaches. Native AI models are stored in Android’s private app sandbox directory, cleartext HTTP traffic is not enabled by IKHO in the Android manifest, and communications performed by Google’s Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs use encrypted network transport managed by those SDKs. However, no software or device environment can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

IKHO is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age under the laws of your jurisdiction), and MistyForgeLabs does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that third-party advertising SDKs within IKHO have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, please contact us using the details below so we can assist or review configuration. You can also delete/reset the device Advertising ID and uninstall the app.

9. Your Privacy & Consent Choices

  • Regional Ad Consent (Google UMP): Where regional regulations require a privacy options entry point, open Settings → PRIVACY & LEGAL → Ad Privacy Choices in IKHO to review, modify, or withdraw your consent preferences at any time.
  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android, go to device Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads.
  • Microphone Permission: You may deny or revoke microphone permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → IKHO → Permissions. Typed text translation and audio playback continue to work without microphone permission.
