Data Deletion Request

IKHO Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to delete data stored locally by the IKHO Android application (com.ikho.mistyforgelabs), developed by MistyForgeLabs, what data is removed when you clear or uninstall the app, and what advertising-related data may still be independently processed by Google under Google’s own policies.

No Accounts or Developer Servers

IKHO has no user accounts or developer-operated cloud servers. Your voice recordings and translations stay on your device, so there is no remote user database held by MistyForgeLabs and no server-side deletion portal is required.

Third-Party Ad SDK Limitation

Banner advertising uses Google AdMob and Google UMP SDKs. MistyForgeLabs cannot access or delete advertising data processed or retained independently by Google under Google’s policies.

1. Overview & Scope

These deletion instructions apply to the IKHO mobile application published by MistyForgeLabs. Core speech recognition, neural machine translation, and speech synthesis run offline on your Android device without sending voice audio or translated text to MistyForgeLabs servers.

For complete details on how IKHO handles local data, permissions, and third-party advertising SDKs, read the full IKHO Privacy Policy.

2. How to Delete Locally Stored Data in IKHO

You can clear IKHO’s locally stored session data directly inside the app at any time:

Data Type In-App Path Effect
Translation history History → Clear Prompts for confirmation and immediately clears your in-session translation history and conversation messages from the History and Translate views.
App settings & locally stored session data Settings → Clear All Opens the confirmation dialog to clear locally held session translation data. Active in-memory app preferences (UI language, speech rate, default direction, script auto-detection) also reset when the app session closes or when app storage is cleared through Android settings.

Temporary synthesized text-to-speech (.wav) audio cache files created for local playback are deleted automatically when another audio clip is played or when the audio player is disposed. Microphone PCM audio is processed in volatile memory (RAM) only and discarded when recording stops or is cancelled.

3. Removing All Local App Storage, Cached Preferences & Models

If you also want to erase cached Google UMP consent preferences stored on your device, temporary cache files, and the extracted offline AI models directory (app_flutter/models, ~857 MB), perform either action in Android:

  • Clear App Data: Open Android Settings → Apps → IKHO → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data.
  • Uninstall IKHO: Uninstall IKHO from your device to remove the app sandbox, local cache, UMP consent storage, and all extracted AI models permanently.

4. What Data Is Deleted vs. What Data May Remain

Data Deleted via In-App Options & Device Removal

  • History → Clear / Settings → Clear All: Deletes locally stored translation history and session phrases immediately inside the running app.
  • Closing / Clearing App Data / Uninstalling: Resets session settings and removes all app sandbox files, temporary TTS .wav cache files, Android SharedPreferences (including local UMP consent state), and extracted AI models on the device.
  • Developer servers: MistyForgeLabs does not operate backend servers or user accounts, so zero copies of your voice audio or translated text exist on MistyForgeLabs systems.

Data That May Remain Independent of Local Deletion

  • Google AdMob & Google UMP SDK Data: When internet connectivity is available and UMP consent requirements permit ad requests, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may independently transmit, process, and retain advertising-related device data (such as Android Advertising ID AD_ID, App Set ID, Privacy Sandbox AdServices signals, IP address / approximate location, ad impressions/clicks, and SDK diagnostics) under Google’s own policies.
  • No Developer Control Over Google Retention: Deleting local translation history or settings via History → Clear, Settings → Clear All, or uninstalling IKHO does not delete data previously received or retained independently by Google or its advertising partners. MistyForgeLabs cannot access, view, or erase data held on Google’s servers.

5. Managing Advertising & Consent Data Controlled by Google

To control or limit advertising data processed independently by Google on your Android device:

  • Review or Withdraw Consent In-App: Where applicable under regional privacy rules (such as EEA/UK/Switzerland GDPR or regulated US state privacy frameworks), open Settings → PRIVACY & LEGAL → Ad Privacy Choices in IKHO to review or update your UMP privacy preferences.
  • Reset or Delete Your Android Advertising ID: Open Android device Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your device Advertising ID.
  • Google Privacy Controls: Review Google’s policies and user data controls at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

6. Retention & Request Limitations

Please note the exact scope of what MistyForgeLabs can and cannot delete upon a data deletion enquiry:

  • No server-side deletion mechanism exists for user audio or translations because MistyForgeLabs never collects, uploads, or stores voice recordings, transcripts, or translations on any server.
  • No account identifier lookup exists because IKHO does not use logins, emails, or user profiles inside the app.
  • Google AdMob / UMP data retention is governed solely by Google’s retention schedules and legal obligations. MistyForgeLabs has no administrative access to delete individual device records stored by Google.
