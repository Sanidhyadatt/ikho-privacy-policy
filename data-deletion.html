IKHO Data Deletion & Ad Privacy Choices

Effective Date: 27 November 2026 · App: IKHO (com.ikho.mistyforgelabs) · Developer: MistyForgeLabs

IKHO does not use accounts, logins, or MistyForgeLabs cloud servers. Translation history and voice audio remain on your device, while advertising is handled independently by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). This page explains how to delete local data, manage ad consent, and understand retention limits.

1. How to Delete Locally Stored Data Inside IKHO

You can delete IKHO’s active local translation and session data directly inside the app:

  1. Translation history: Open History → Clear and confirm to erase your active conversation history and translated phrases from the History and Translate screens.
  2. App settings and locally stored session data: Open Settings → Clear All and confirm to clear locally held session data. Active in-memory preferences (UI language, playback speed, default language direction, and auto-detection toggle) also clear when the app process closes or when app storage is cleared in Android.

Microphone PCM audio stays strictly in RAM and is discarded immediately when recording stops or cancels. Temporary synthesized text-to-speech .wav files in the local cache directory are deleted automatically on subsequent playback or player disposal.

2. Complete On-Device Storage & UMP Cache Removal

If you also want to erase locally cached Google UMP consent preferences stored in Android SharedPreferences, temporary cache files, and extracted AI models (app_flutter/models, ~857 MB):

  • Clear storage in Android: Go to device Settings → Apps → IKHO → Storage & cache → Clear storage / Clear data.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling IKHO deletes the entire private app directory, settings, UMP consent state, and extracted model files from your phone.

3. What Data Is Deleted vs. What Data May Remain

Data Category Action Result
Locally stored translation history & session settings History → Clear, Settings → Clear All, or closing / clearing app data Deleted from active app memory and local session state. MistyForgeLabs holds zero remote copies.
Cached UMP consent flags, temp .wav files & local AI models Android Clear storage / Clear data or uninstalling IKHO Removed completely from your Android device.
Google AdMob & Google UMP advertising data Local IKHO clearing or uninstalling May remain with Google: Advertising IDs (AD_ID), IP/coarse location, ad impressions, and diagnostics already sent while online remain subject to Google’s independent policies.

4. Ad Privacy Choices & Google Advertising Controls

When network access is available and regional privacy regulations require consent management, IKHO uses Google UMP and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDKs. You can manage advertising data directly via:

  • In-App Ad Privacy Choices: Where applicable under regional rules (such as EEA/UK/Switzerland GDPR or regulated US states), open Settings → PRIVACY & LEGAL → Ad Privacy Choices inside IKHO to review, modify, or withdraw ad consent at any time.
  • Android Advertising ID controls: Go to Android device Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Google privacy documentation: Review Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Applicable Retention Limitations

MistyForgeLabs aims to be clear about what we cannot access or erase:

  • No server-side deletion portal exists because MistyForgeLabs does not operate accounts or cloud servers that store your voice recordings, transcripts, or translations.
  • MistyForgeLabs does not directly control Google’s retention: Google AdMob and UMP process and retain advertising telemetry independently under Google’s own systems and policies. MistyForgeLabs cannot query, export, or delete Google’s server-side records for individual devices.
